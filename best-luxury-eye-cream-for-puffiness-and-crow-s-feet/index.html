Where the evidence stops

An acid-containing eye product deserves a careful reading of the label. The American Academy of Dermatology notes that glycolic acid can irritate skin, particularly sensitive skin. “Scent-free” also does not establish suitability for a particular fragrance allergy.

Before you buy

Follow the current eye-area directions rather than adapting a face-acid routine. Compare the full ingredient list with products you already use and confirm return conditions. If your skin is reactive or you are unsure about combining actives, discuss the formula with a dermatologist.

For skin-care tolerance questions, the American Academy of Dermatology’s product-testing guide is a useful general reference. It does not endorse this product.
