What to look for in Skincare

Lean mineral over chemical for reactive skin. Sensitive and compromised skin often flares with chemical UV filters. This pick uses a mineral blend instead, which sits on the surface and deflects UV rather than absorbing it. That's usually the gentler path for an easily irritated face.
Confirm it's broad-spectrum. SPF on its own only describes UVB protection. You want broad-spectrum, which also covers UVA (the rays tied to aging and longer-term damage). This SmartBlend SPF 75 is labeled broad-spectrum, so both are handled.
Decide if you want SPF and moisturizer combined. This is a mineral moisturizer with sun protection in it. If you'd rather keep steps separate, a standalone SPF over your usual cream works fine too. A combined formula just gives sensitive skin one fewer layer to react to.
Look for redness control if you flush easily. A color-correcting tint that cancels redness is a real help for rosacea-prone or reactive skin, since it evens out tone while it protects. Just check that the tint reads well on your own skin tone before you commit.
