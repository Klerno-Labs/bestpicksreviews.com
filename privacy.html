Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

BestPicks Reviews ("we," "us," or "our") operates this website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our site. Please read this policy carefully. By using the site, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Website delivery and optional analytics

Our hosting and content-delivery providers receive technical request information, such as an IP address and browser information, when serving a page. Some guides load merchant product images or fonts from third-party servers; those servers also receive a request when the resource loads.

Optional Google Analytics is separate and loads only after you allow it. It can collect page visits, device and browser information, approximate location, referral information and events such as outbound affiliate-link clicks. The site works when you decline analytics.

Information You Provide

If you contact us via email, we collect the information you voluntarily provide, such as your name, email address, and the content of your message.

Cookie Usage

Optional Google Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” You can decline or change your choice using “Privacy choices” in the footer. We store that choice in your browser. The site and product links work without analytics. Technologies used include:

  • Site preferences -- local browser storage remembers your analytics choice and, on some pages, a display-theme preference
  • Analytics cookies -- help us understand how visitors interact with our site (via Google Analytics)
  • Affiliate tracking cookies -- used by Awin affiliate partners and other affiliate programs to attribute purchases

You can clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings. Clearing the stored analytics choice means we will ask again. Changing your choice to decline stops future site analytics collection but does not automatically remove cookies already stored; you can remove those through browser settings.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may collect information about you:

  • Awin affiliate partners -- when you click affiliate links, the affiliate network and merchant may use cookies or other tracking for commission attribution, subject to their own notices and choices. Their respective privacy policies apply to data collected on their platform.
  • Google Analytics -- collects usage information after you opt in, including page visits and affiliate-link clicks with the article, network, advertiser ID, and link placement. This measures clicks, not purchases. We disable advertising signals in our tag configuration and do not include the page query string in our configured page location. Google processes data under its own policies. You can opt out using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

  • Operate and maintain the website
  • Analyze usage patterns and improve our content
  • Track affiliate link performance
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Third-party analytics providers as described above
  • Law enforcement if required by law or to protect our rights

Data Retention

As checked September 27, 2026, our Google Analytics property is configured to retain event-level data for 2 months and user-level data for 14 months, with user retention reset on new activity. These settings do not apply to most aggregated reports. Hosting providers and merchants have separate retention policies. Information you send by email is used to handle your inquiry; contact us to request access or deletion.

Your Rights (CCPA / GDPR)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to know -- request details about what personal information we collect and how it is used
  • Right to delete -- request deletion of your personal information
  • Right to opt out -- opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (we do not sell data)
  • Right to non-discrimination -- we will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights
  • Right to access and portability -- request a copy of your personal data in a portable format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@bestpicksreviews.com.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected data from a child, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@bestpicksreviews.com.
