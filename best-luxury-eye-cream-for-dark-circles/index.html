When it is worth considering

Shortlist ILUMA if you want an eye cream with this brand’s brightening positioning. We cannot promise a change in dark circles, the time it would take, or that it will outperform a moisturizer you already use.

Where the evidence stops

The merchant uses strong language about discoloration and a youthful appearance. We have not treated those statements as proof of pigment correction or a clinical treatment. A cosmetic label does not explain an individual reader’s under-eye appearance.

Before you buy

Check the exact formula on the current package and the eye-area directions. Decide whether your priority is a cream texture, hydration or a specific ingredient. Confirm the amount and return terms before comparing prices; an expensive jar is not evidence of better results.

For skin-care tolerance questions, the American Academy of Dermatology’s product-testing guide is a useful general reference. It does not endorse this product.
