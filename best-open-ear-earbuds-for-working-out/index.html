What makes this model different

Adjustment is a feature to evaluate.

OpenRock describes a pivot that adjusts 45° left/right and 50° up/down. That gives you positioning options to investigate; it does not prove that the X holds through sprints, jumps or a cycling session. The manual’s wearing sequence shows how to move the earbud relative to the hook.

  1. Match the fit to the exercise.

    Before a permitted try-on, identify the movement that usually dislodges your earbuds. Check the interaction with glasses and headgear. We would not pay for extra adjustment without a way to establish that it helps your fit.

  2. Make controls part of the decision.

    The manual lists button controls for playback, calls, tracks and volume, with customization through the app. We have not tested wet-finger operation, access while wearing gloves, or accidental presses.

  3. Keep environmental awareness a separate question.

    We have not measured audibility of surrounding sounds. An open-ear design does not guarantee that you will hear a hazard or make road use safe. Do not choose this product on that promise.

Plan around the earbud charge

The case total is not one continuous workout.

OpenRock states that the X’s playback figures were measured at 70% volume in its laboratory, with temperature specified as 25°C for the battery/charging tests. We have not repeated that procedure. Your volume, calls and charging habits may differ.

Use the single-charge figure when planning time away from the case. Leave room for your own usage instead of scheduling a long outing around a manufacturer maximum. We have not verified a separate continuous-call duration.

The manual lists AAC and SBC codecs, Bluetooth 5.3, and a second-device pairing procedure. It does not establish that your particular phone/watch combination will switch without interruption. Confirm that use case before purchase.

Claims that need a real test

Bass, leakage and sweat durability remain unmeasured.

The earlier guide treated bass branding as evidence that the X outperformed other open-ear earbuds. We do not have a listening comparison or frequency-response measurements to support that. Likewise, microphone processing does not establish either voice clarity in wind or quieter listening for the wearer.

The table records the earbud water rating without extending it to the case or years of sweat exposure. Follow the care instructions and clarify coverage for your use. A protection label and a durability test answer different questions.

A future useful workout comparison would record fit changes with normal headgear, button access during repeatable movement, leakage at a stated distance and volume, and playback duration under the same conditions for each model. No such test is represented here.

Purchase terms

Check trial eligibility before training.

OpenRock’s return policy requires an unworn or unused item, original accessories and packaging, and proof of purchase within its 30-day request window. Non-quality returns leave transport costs with the buyer. Confirm eligibility before using a pair for exercise.

The separate warranty describes 24-month coverage with exclusions and one free defective-headphone replacement. Neither policy is our evidence of workout performance.
