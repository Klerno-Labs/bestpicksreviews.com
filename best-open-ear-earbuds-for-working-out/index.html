Fit security comes first. When you're working out, a bud that stays put beats everything else. The OpenRock X uses hooks that pivot 45 degrees left and right and 50 degrees up and down, so you can match the angle to your own ears instead of hoping a one-size hook survives sprints and burpees.

Awareness versus isolation. Open-ear air-conduction buds let outside sound in on purpose. On outdoor runs and in busy gyms that's exactly what you want, since you need to hear what's around you. Yes, and that's the main reason to go with this style. Open-ear air-conduction buds sit outside your ear canal instead of plugging it, so you stay aware of traffic, people, and the gym while you train. The catch is less isolation and more leakage than sealed in-ears.
