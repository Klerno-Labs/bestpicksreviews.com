When to consider it

Shortlist this if you want to compare a cabin layout and can establish that the precise configuration fits your site. Four-person capacity and six-foot wall height are marketing or option labels, not a measurement of usable comfort or final installed clearance.

Limits of this review

The current six variants all include a named heater. We found no heater-free variant in the checked product data, so the old heater-excluded pricing explanation has been removed. We have not verified heat-up performance, assembly effort, insulation performance or weather durability.

Before ordering

Ask for an itemized quote that specifies the enclosure, heater, controller, stones, roof and delivery service. Obtain complete exterior and interior dimensions rather than planning from wall height. Clarify foundation, drainage, assembly, electrical work, permits where applicable, cancellation terms and freight-damage procedures. A qualified installer should check the relevant manuals and your actual site before the order is finalized.

Prices, stock and delivery dates can change. Confirm the exact variant and complete delivered and installed cost; this guide does not provide a live quote.

An alternative to compare

The Sweat Barrel offers a different footprint and seating shape, with a Regular two-person option. Compare the dimensioned layouts and total installation costs rather than choosing by occupant count alone. A lower-capacity enclosure may be sufficient if you rarely plan to use it with a group.
