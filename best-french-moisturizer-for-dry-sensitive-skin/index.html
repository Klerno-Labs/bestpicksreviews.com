When it is worth considering

Consider this only if you specifically want an overnight mask and its directions fit your existing routine. The current listing is not a basis for calling it the best everyday moisturizer for sensitive skin.

Where the evidence stops

PDRN in a name is not a clinical result. The listing’s descriptions of softer or more radiant skin are manufacturer claims. We have not reviewed an independent trial of this finished formula or tested its feel, absorption or tolerance.

Before you buy

Confirm the current formulation and amount with the seller because the original page described a different category. Read the complete ingredient list, usage directions and opened-product return conditions. “French” and “luxury” describe positioning; they do not establish that a formula will agree with your skin.

For skin-care tolerance questions, the American Academy of Dermatology’s product-testing guide is a useful general reference. It does not endorse this product.
