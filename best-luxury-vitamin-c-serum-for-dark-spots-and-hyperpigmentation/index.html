IMAGE MD power-C serum: compare the vitamin C forms

IMAGE lists 20% tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate. MISSHA lists a percentage of a liposome formula. Those percentages refer to different things and do not establish a potency ranking.

IMAGE formula claim20% tetrahexyldecyl ascorbate, plus acetyl zingerone and a hydrating antioxidant complex.
MISSHA formula claim33% Vita C Liposome Formula containing vitamin C described as 99% pure.
Important distinctionNeither statement means that the finished products contain 20% and 33% L-ascorbic acid respectively.

These are dated listing checks, not laboratory results. Formulas, package sizes, price and availability can change; confirm the exact product before ordering.

When it is worth considering

Compare these if you are considering a vitamin C serum and want to understand what each label actually states. We have not measured pigment changes or compared stability, irritation or effectiveness of the finished formulas.

Where the evidence stops

The larger number is not automatically the stronger product. IMAGE names a vitamin C derivative; MISSHA gives a carrier-formula percentage and ingredient-purity claim. We removed the old implication that these numbers could support a simple dark-spot performance ranking.

Before you buy

Read the complete ingredients, storage directions and current packaging. Ask the manufacturer for evidence tied to the exact formula if fading a particular type of mark is the reason you are buying. This guide cannot diagnose pigmentation or promise that a cosmetic will treat it.

For skin-care tolerance questions, the American Academy of Dermatology’s product-testing guide is a useful general reference. It does not endorse this product.

A comparison to make

IMAGE MD restoring youth serum is a different combination that names vitamin C and peptides without a concentration in the checked short description. If a known concentration is essential to your choice, do not assume it shares power-C’s formula. Choosing no new serum is reasonable when your existing routine already meets your needs.
