The question behind this page

Will they stay on your ears?

We cannot promise that. OpenRock describes a hook-clip system and eight contact points, but that description does not establish what happens on your ears during a run. Treat the S2 as a candidate for a fit check, not a guaranteed solution to earbuds falling out.

  1. Check the contact points with your usual gear.

    If the seller permits a try-on, use the glasses, hat or helmet you normally wear. Look for pressure, rubbing and interference rather than assuming a light earbud will disappear from notice.

  2. Use the maker’s wearing guide, then assess movement.

    Follow the fitting diagram before judging retention. A permitted indoor try-on can reveal obvious slipping during head turns; it cannot establish race-day stability or hours of comfort.

  3. Decide what evidence you need before spending.

    If repeated movement or a long workout is essential to your decision, seek an actual documented fit report or a suitable trial policy. We have not performed that test.

Separate features from results

LDAC is a compatibility question, not a sound-quality verdict.

Choose around your actual phone, watch or laptop. We have not verified LDAC operation with each device or whether every codec/multipoint combination is supported. Ask specifically about the combination you will use; a Bluetooth version alone does not answer that question.

The advertised four-microphone processing concerns calls. We have no controlled wind-noise recording, leakage measurement or listening comparison, so we cannot translate the feature list into a promise of clear outdoor calls or stronger bass.

Leaving the ear canal open does not establish that you will hear a particular warning, vehicle or person while audio is playing. This guide makes no traffic-safety guarantee.

Before the first try-on

Read the return conditions first.

OpenRock allows a return request within 30 days of receipt but requires the item to be unworn or unused, with original packaging, accessories and proof of purchase. Non-quality return transport is the buyer’s cost. Read the current policy before assuming a workout trial is covered.

The warranty describes 24 months and one free defective-headphone replacement, with exclusions. Confirm care instructions and a fit-return arrangement before opening the order.
